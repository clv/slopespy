    About Slope Spy

    Two ski resorts, side by side, at exactly the same scale: both halves share one camera, so a kilometre on the left is a kilometre on the right. Tap a resort's name to pick another.

    Drag to orbit, right-drag or two fingers to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Hover or tap a lift for details.

    Heights are drawn at 2× so slopes read clearly. Trees and buildings stay at their true height.

    Data

    • Ski areas, runs, lifts: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via OpenSkiMap
    • Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM, USGS 3DEP, Copernicus and others; attribution)
    • Trees and land cover: © ESA WorldCover project 2021 (CC BY 4.0)
    • Buildings, roads, parking: Overture Maps (ODbL)

    Resorts are ranked by mapped run length, so how complete each one looks depends on OpenStreetMap volunteers.
